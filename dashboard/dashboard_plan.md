# Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Dashboard Purpose

The dashboard will provide a visual summary of the sales
performance of the business.

## Key Metrics

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value

## Charts

### 1. Sales by Product
Shows which products generate the most sales.

### 2. Sales by Region
Shows sales performance across different regions.

### 3. Monthly Sales
Shows how sales change over time.

### 4. Profit by Product
Shows which products generate the most profit.

## Future Dashboard

The final version can be developed using:

- Power BI
- Python
- Matplotlib

## Business Questions

The dashboard should help answer:

1. What are the total sales?
2. What is the total profit?
3. Which product generates the most sales?
4. Which product generates the most profit?
5. Which region performs best?
6. Which month has the highest sales?
7. What is the average order value?
